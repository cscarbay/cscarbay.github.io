About Our Founder

Solangie Martínez

Founder - General Manager

Solangie "Sol" Martinez is a Mechanical Maintenance Engineer with over 21 years of experience in the execution of industrial and energy projects. Her career spans more than 25 capital investment initiatives—15 of them in Venezuela’s oil sector and the rest in the United States—allowing her to integrate technical and strategic approaches across diverse, challenging, and multicultural environments.

Trained in Venezuela, Eng. Martinez faced additional layers of complexity in project execution, including remote site locations, reliance on imported equipment, and sociopolitical volatility. These conditions sharpened her ability to anticipate risks, manage budgets and schedules with precision, and develop durable, efficient solutions in highly constrained environments.

Her systematic thinking and multidisciplinary approach have led to significant cost savings in industrial projects. She has developed and implemented methodologies for equipment criticality assessment and spare parts inventory systems tailored to commissioning and the first years of operation—bridging two of her core professional passions: Project Management and Mechanical Maintenance.

Solangie firmly believes in mentorship as a transformative tool to uncover individual talents and place people in environments where they can thrive. Her perspective on the fossil energy industry recognizes its resilient and productive role in paving the way for the adoption of clean and renewable energy sources—essential for planetary sustainability and the future of coming generations.

With this solid technical, strategic, and human foundation, Solangie Martinez founded Sustainable Management Consulting, LLC on August 6, 2025, with the mission to deliver intelligent, sustainable, replicable, and adaptable solutions that strengthen industrial infrastructure resilience, create tangible value for clients and communities, and contribute to nation-building and the development of future professionals.
