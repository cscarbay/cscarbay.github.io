About Us

At D’ HOY Distribution South Florida Inc. we are dedicated to the sale and distribution of fresh vegetables in South Florida, offering high-quality products to restaurants, retail stores, whole food businesses and individual consumers.

Our commitment is to guarantee freshness, flavor, and reliable service, bringing the finest products from the field to your table.

We are key allies for those who value natural, fresh, and well-selected ingredients.

Our Mission:

Offering fresh fruits and vegetables of the highest quality, ensuring efficient and timely distribution to wholesalers, supermarkets, and restaurants. We work hand in hand with local and international producers to bring freshness from the field to the table, promoting agricultural development and providing reliable service.

Our Vision:

To be a leading fresh produce distributor, recognized for its excellence in quality, logistics, and service. We aim to be the preferred partner for those seeking freshness, trust, and a partner in their growth.
